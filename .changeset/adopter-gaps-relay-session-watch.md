Realtime, session checks and uploads for apps that keep the session out of the browser, and atomic multi-limit rate limiting.

- `realtimeRelay(server, { topics, postgres })` from `better-supabase/server` serves Realtime to callers without a browser token of their own, such as a support session ("view as user") or an app with httpOnly session cookies. It checks each topic's `realtime.messages` policies as the caller (the viewed user in a support session), joins upstream with the secret key and streams broadcasts and presence as server-sent events, with heartbeats, reconnects, session expiry and an overflow limit. `POST` broadcasts for the caller and is refused in a read-only support session. `liveRelayTopic` serves the `liveQuery` channels, and `realtimeTopicAccess` runs the policy check on its own.
- `relayRealtimeClient(url)` and `relayTopicPort(url)` from `better-supabase/realtime` are the client: `defineTopic(...).subscribe`, `send`, `liveQuery`, `watchTopic` and the hooks take it unchanged. `<BetterSupabaseProvider realtime={...}>` makes `useBroadcast`, `usePresence`, `useLiveQuery` and `useLiveCount` join through it, and `useRealtimeRelay(url)` returns one while `useSupportSession()` reports a support session. The hooks' `client` options now take any `RealtimeClient`.
- `sessionStatusRoute(server)` from `better-supabase/server` and `useSessionWatch()` (plus the framework-free `watchSession()`) from `better-supabase/next/client` watch an open page without a browser Supabase client: on focus, visibility and an interval they report an ended, banned or signed-out session and a changed claims fingerprint (support target included), and tell the other tabs through a `BroadcastChannel`. `claimsFingerprint()` computes the fingerprint on the server.
- `bucket.reserveResumable(target, { upsert, direct })` signs a resumable (TUS) upload and returns `tusEndpoint`, `headers` (`x-signature`, `x-upsert`), `metadata`, `bucket` and `expiresAt`. The endpoint is `<storage>/upload/resumable/sign`, on the direct `<project-ref>.storage.supabase.co` host for hosted projects; `resumableEndpoint(url)` builds it on its own. `claimReserved` and support-session checks work as for `reserve`.
- `createRateLimit().checkAll([{ scope, key, rule }, ...])` counts one hit against several limits in one round trip through the new `check_rate_limits(hits)` function of the `rate-limit` module. The hit counts only when every limit allows it, and the decision names the limit that blocked and the longest `retryAfter`. Run `better-supabase upgrade` to add the function.
