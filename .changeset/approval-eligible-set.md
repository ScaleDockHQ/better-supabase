The approvals block adds `approval_eligible_ids_in(tenants)`, the pending requests the caller may decide in several tenants, or in every tenant when null, from one call. Each permission is checked once through `tenant_ids_with` instead of one `can` call per tenant and permission, and a block's `selfDecides` rule runs only for the caller's own requests (the expenses block answers them with one `expense_self_decides_many` call). The function stays `stable` and writes no settings. `approval_eligible_ids(tenant)` calls it and keeps its signature. With 60 tenants and 2,400 pending requests, a caller who owns 14 of them went from 444 `can` calls and 122 ms for three scans of the requests in one statement (one call per member tenant plus one without a tenant) to 12.7 ms with one `approval_eligible_ids_in()` call per scan, or 5.8 ms with the member tenants passed as an array. Run `better-supabase upgrade` and generate a migration, then replace a policy that calls `approval_eligible_ids` once per member organization and again without a tenant with one `id in (select better_supabase.approval_eligible_ids_in())`. Approvals module version 7, no data step.
