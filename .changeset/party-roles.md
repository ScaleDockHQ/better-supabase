Companies and people in the `contacts` block can carry roles, payment terms, a credit limit and a supplier status. The block adds `roles text[]` (checked against `blocks.contacts.options.roles`, default `customer`, `supplier`, `funder` and `partner`), `payment_terms_days`, `credit_limit` with `credit_currency` (a `Money` value in the client) and `supplier_status` (`approved`, `conditional` or `blocked`). `set_party_roles(party, roles)` replaces a party's roles. `set_supplier_status(party, status, reason)` needs the new `contacts.approve_supplier` permission, records an audit entry and emits `company.supplier_status_changed` or `person.supplier_status_changed` with the previous status and the reason. When the `qualifications` block is installed, `supplier_status_from_qualifications(party)` returns `approved` for a supplier with no missing or expired qualification and `conditional` otherwise; pass the result to `set_supplier_status` to keep the approved supplier list of ISO 9001 clause 8.4. `list_companies` and `list_people` take `role` and `supplier_status` filters. The client adds `db.contacts.setRoles()`, `setSupplierStatus()`, `supplierStatusFromQualifications()` and the `role` and `supplierStatus` options on `listCompanies()` and `listPeople()`. Grant `contacts.approve_supplier` to the roles that approve suppliers, then run `better-supabase upgrade` and generate a migration to pick up the columns and functions.
