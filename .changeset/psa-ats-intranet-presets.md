Three new named presets: `psa` (professional services: clients, tasks, time tracked against rate cards, a staffing roster, expenses, quotes, contracts and invoices), `ats` (applicant tracking: hiring pipelines, application forms, interview scheduling, offers with signatures) and `intranet` (announcements, knowledge base, drive, channels, staff directory and policy acknowledgements).

Use them with `npx better-supabase init --preset psa` or `defineConfig({ preset: "psa" })`. HR operations stay the `people` preset and the help desk stays `helpdesk`.
