Add `better-supabase completion bash|zsh|fish`. It prints a script that
completes commands, subcommands, options and their values (block names,
presets, `gen --only` tasks, doctor rule codes) for both `better-supabase` and
`bsb`. Add `eval "$(better-supabase completion zsh)"` to `~/.zshrc`, or the
line for your shell from the [shell completion](https://bettersupabase.com/docs/cli/completion)
page.
