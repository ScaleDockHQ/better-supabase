`defineBlock` takes `composes` and `defaults` to build a block from other blocks. `createBlocks` hands the composite its parts' clients as `options.parts`, `resolvePreset` installs the parts with the reason `part of <composite>` and returns `partOptions`, `removeComposite` removes a composite with or without its parts, and the catalog lists `composes`, `defaults` and `composedBy`.
