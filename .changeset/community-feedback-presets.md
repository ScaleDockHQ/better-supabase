`better-supabase/preset` adds the `community` and `feedback` presets to the `verticals` group, both on the `posts` block. `community` is `saas` plus forum boards of voted posts, messaging and channels, comments, reactions, moderation, post scores, tags and search. `feedback` is `saas` plus feedback boards of voted ideas with roadmap statuses, comments, reactions, moderation, work items linked to the ideas they ship through relations, tags and search. Each has a page under [Presets](https://bettersupabase.com/docs/presets). Existing presets and preset codes keep their blocks.
