Blocks sit in 16 categories instead of 10, so the catalog, `blocks list --category` and `/blocks` group them the way an app is built. `BlockCategory` takes the new ids:

| Old category                 | New category                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| ---------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `foundation`                 | `identity` (access, actor, identities, mfa, sessions, sso, subjects), `tenancy` (tenant, organizations, teams, profiles, settings, reserved-slugs) and `database` (updated-at)                                                                                                                |
| `collaboration`              | `collaboration`, and `messaging` (notifications, push, inbox)                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| `work`                       | `work`, `people` (employees, hiring), `identity` (access-requests) and `database` (rank)                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| `scheduling`                 | `scheduling`, and `people` (time-tracking)                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| `operations`                 | `growth` (onboarding, waitlist, announcements, flags), `admin` (audit, data-lifecycle, metrics, support-sessions) and `automation` (workflows, workflow-builder, workflow-sdk-world)                                                                                                          |
| `platform`                   | `developer` (api-keys, credentials, service principals, signing keys, shared signals, the webhook blocks, rate-limit, idempotency), `automation` (jobs, outbox, streams), `identity` (grants) and `database` (ensure-rls, pgtap, jsonb-schemas, number-sequences, read-sets, realtime-tables) |
| `ai`                         | `ai`, and `automation` (connectors)                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| `crm`, `commerce`, `content` | unchanged                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |

`better-supabase/preset` adds 14 named presets, so `/create` and `init` offer 22 in five groups (`PRESET_GROUPS`, `NamedPreset.group`): `consumer` (per-user data with `scope: "user"`), `helpdesk`, `marketplace`, `people`, `drive`, `docs`, `invoicing`, `forms`, `ai-chat`, `ai-agents`, `developer-api`, `automation`, `enterprise` and `admin`. Every preset after the starters builds on `saas`. The existing presets keep their blocks, and `NamedPreset.conventions` holds the conventions a preset sets over the defaults.
