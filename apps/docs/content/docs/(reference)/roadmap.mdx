The roadmap groups work by horizon. Each item names the upstream project it
follows and the subpath where it lands, so you can tell which import changes
when it ships. Items move between horizons as upstream releases land; the
[changelog](/docs/changelog)
records what shipped.

## Shipped for 0.47

These are merged on `main` and ship in 0.47.0. The
[0.46 to 0.47 upgrade guide](/docs/migration/0.46-to-0.47) covers the
breaking ones.

- **Quality management** (the [qms](/docs/presets/qms),
  [ehs](/docs/presets/ehs) and [isms](/docs/presets/isms) presets): the
  [acknowledgements](/docs/blocks/acknowledgements),
  [cases](/docs/blocks/cases), [inspections](/docs/blocks/inspections),
  [risks](/docs/blocks/risks) and
  [qualifications](/docs/blocks/qualifications) blocks, drive roles, file
  requests and controlled documents numbered per type, all linked through
  `relations` kinds. The [quality management](/docs/guides/quality-management)
  guide walks ISO 9001 clause by clause. See
  [ADR 0132](https://github.com/ScaleDockHQ/better-supabase/blob/main/docs/decisions/0132-quality-blocks.md).
- **Care records** (the [care](/docs/presets/care) preset): record teams,
  emergency access and a read log with the NEN 7513 fields, encrypted
  lookups, a subject export, representatives,
  [consents](/docs/blocks/consents), progress notes,
  [care plans](/docs/blocks/care-plans),
  [observations](/docs/blocks/observations),
  [medications](/docs/blocks/medications) with signatures and guest reads,
  [clinical decision support](/docs/blocks/clinical-decision-support) alerts,
  [e-prescribing](/docs/blocks/e-prescribing),
  [funding](/docs/blocks/funding), [shifts](/docs/blocks/shifts) and
  [FHIR R4 mappers](/docs/concepts/fhir). See
  [ADR 0134](https://github.com/ScaleDockHQ/better-supabase/blob/main/docs/decisions/0134-record-teams-access-and-read-logging.md)
  and
  [ADR 0135](https://github.com/ScaleDockHQ/better-supabase/blob/main/docs/decisions/0135-care-records.md).
- **ERP** (the [erp](/docs/presets/erp) and
  [manufacturing](/docs/presets/manufacturing) presets): the
  [accounting](/docs/blocks/accounting) books beside the ledger, with
  postings from invoices, charges, write-offs and named instances; purchase
  bills, requisitions, receipts and credit limits; [banking](/docs/blocks/banking)
  with statement import and reconciliation; collections, dunning and UBL
  e-invoices; [manufacturing](/docs/blocks/manufacturing), whose purchase
  suggestions go through purchasing; fixed asset depreciation, project costing
  and [legal entities](/docs/blocks/legal-entities). See
  [ADR 0136](https://github.com/ScaleDockHQ/better-supabase/blob/main/docs/decisions/0136-accounting-books-beside-the-ledger.md).
- **Meetings and payroll export**: the [meetings](/docs/blocks/meetings) block
  (agenda, attendees, minutes, decisions, votes and action items as tasks)
  and the [payroll export](/docs/blocks/payroll-export) block with its
  `PayrollExportFormat` port.
- **Part 2 blocks:** [maintenance](/docs/blocks/maintenance) with meter
  readings and work orders, [rfqs](/docs/blocks/rfqs) for requests for
  quotation, [appraisals](/docs/blocks/appraisals),
  [warehouse](/docs/blocks/warehouse) pick waves and cycle counts,
  [tills](/docs/blocks/tills) with cash counts and bank deposits,
  [dam](/docs/blocks/dam) renditions and usage rights, and
  [lims](/docs/blocks/lims) samples, methods and results.
- **Part 2 platform work:** the versioned `RouteOptimizer` port on
  [dispatch](/docs/blocks/dispatch), offline sync for care records through
  PowerSync, the `psa`, `ats` and `intranet` presets, and on
  [drive](/docs/blocks/drive) a retention sweep per label, a changes feed and
  a WOPI host.
- **One naming convention** (breaking): entity keys equal the entity's
  singular, event types are `<entity>.<past verb>`, module tables and the task
  and activity foreign key columns follow their entity, inbox contacts are
  contacts people, sequence runs are enrollments, AI file versions live in
  `versions`, postmortem actions are tasks and policy acknowledgements live in
  `acknowledgements`. See
  [ADR 0130](https://github.com/ScaleDockHQ/better-supabase/blob/main/docs/decisions/0130-one-naming-convention-for-blocks.md)
  and
  [ADR 0131](https://github.com/ScaleDockHQ/better-supabase/blob/main/docs/decisions/0131-merge-overlapping-blocks.md).
- **Block overlaps decided**: each overlapping pair stays separate with a
  stated job, and seven blocks moved to the matching docs group. See
  [ADR 0150](https://github.com/ScaleDockHQ/better-supabase/blob/main/docs/decisions/0150-block-overlaps.md).

## Now

Shipping in 0.9.

- **Exact money on the wire** (the deals, quotes, invoices, pipelines and
  employees blocks, and `db.$rpc`): amounts are `Money` values, every `int8`
  crosses PostgREST as text and decodes strictly, and doctor BS223 flags
  functions that return a bare `bigint` or `numeric`. See
  [ADR 0037](https://github.com/ScaleDockHQ/better-supabase/blob/main/docs/decisions/0037-money-on-the-wire.md).
- **Courses and scored forms** (`better-supabase/blocks/courses` and
  `better-supabase/blocks/forms`): courses of modules and lessons on
  enrollments, quizzes graded in SQL, certificates, and NPS rollups per form.
  See [Courses](/docs/blocks/courses).
- **Posts** (`better-supabase/blocks/posts`): forum and feedback boards on
  the messaging core, with votes, roadmap statuses, merged duplicates,
  replies and moderation. See [Posts](/docs/blocks/posts).
- **Vertical presets** (`better-supabase/preset`): nineteen presets in a
  Verticals group, from property management on leases to community and
  product feedback on posts, each `saas` plus the blocks of one kind of
  business app. See [Presets](/docs/presets#vertical-presets).

## Planned: collaboration and SaaS parity

This track adds one messaging core with a team chat block on it, moves
comments and the inbox onto that core, and then closes the feature gaps
against CRMs (Salesforce, HubSpot, Attio, Apollo), Google Workspace and
thirty other SaaS verticals. Each phase ships as its own pull request, and
the breaking phases ship with a data migration, an upgrade guide and a
changeset that lists every old name next to its replacement.
[ADR 0062](https://github.com/ScaleDockHQ/better-supabase/blob/main/docs/decisions/0062-messaging-core-and-breaking-moves.md)
records the messaging core and
[ADR 0063](https://github.com/ScaleDockHQ/better-supabase/blob/main/docs/decisions/0063-activity-block.md)
records the activity block.

| Record mentions (smart chips)                        | users only                                                                                     | `@[Title](subject:type/id)` checked with the subject's permission    |

Twenty more verticals (property management, field service, clinics,
courses, events, legal, expenses, contracts, customer success, communities,
product feedback, call intelligence, logistics, restaurants, fitness,
nonprofits, IT service management, compliance, construction and insurance)
need the same few primitives: a ledger, assets and locations, votes and
scores, moderation, enrollments, guest and portal access, check-in passes,
merge and anchors on files. Those become shared blocks first, and the
verticals become thin blocks and presets on top of them.

### Phases

1. **Table and entity renames in `upgrade`:** renaming a block's tables
   or entities on an installed project emits `alter table ... rename` and
   function swaps, plus the column moves a module declares in
   `NAMES.migrations`.
2. **Entities for the collaboration blocks:** comments, attachments,
   reactions, engagements, documents, calendar and reminders declare
   renameable entities and `schemas`, and the hand-written subject cases go.
3. **One `RecordRef` (breaking):** every subject block takes the subject as
   its first argument, reads the tenant from the block environment and
   returns comment and file authors. `createBlockClient` replaces the
   per-block `connect*` wrappers.
4. **Permissions and subjects on the client:** `my_permissions`,
   `permissionFlags`, `can`, `usePermission` and `Can`; `subject_titles`
   and `resolveSubjects`; URLs for every subject.
5. **Sections:** `defineSection` in block manifests, `loadSections`,
   `record_summary`, tenant-scoped cache tags and `createRecordController`.
6. **Generated features:** `gen` writes one binding per subject with a
   section per installed block. UI stays recipes only.
7. **Lists, workspace, flows and forms:** `defineRecordList`, `my_work`,
   generated navigation and commands, `defineFlow` and `recordForm`.
8. **The messaging core and the `channels` block:** conversations,
   messages, edits, members, thread reads, follows, mentions, typing and
   scheduled send, with attachments and reactions on messages, and a `chat`
   preset.
9. **Comments on the core (breaking):** comments become subject
   conversations with resolve, edit history, pins, anchors and attachments,
   and the activity feed moves to its own `timeline` block.
10. **Tasks and files:** attachment versions and previews, checklists,
    recurrence and watchers on work items.
11. **The inbox on the core (breaking):** inbox conversations and messages
    move onto the core, inbound files become scanned attachments, and the
    `crm` preset gains deals, quotes and targets.
12. **Shared collaboration features:** presence, record mentions, save for
    later and AI thread summaries.
13. **CRM gaps:** mail sync, email templates and tracking, sequences,
    segments, assignment and territories, enrichment, lead scoring,
    forecast snapshots and field history.
14. **Google Workspace parity:** calendar availability, appointment
    schedules, inbox snooze and scheduled send, document suggestion mode and
    drive labels and approvals.
15. **Shared primitives (breaking where they absorb existing tables):**
    ledger, assets and locations, votes and scores, moderation, enrollments,
    guest and portal access, merge, file anchors, passes and billable time.
16. **Community and feedback:** the `posts` block, and the `community` and
    `feedback` presets on it. Shipped; see [Posts](/docs/blocks/posts) and
    [Presets](/docs/presets#vertical-presets).

## Planned: block instances and one data contract

Picking blocks should build a working product. This track gives every
concept one name, lets you install a block more than once under its own
names, attaches blocks to your record types by default, and puts app tables
and block tables behind the same data verbs and generated hooks. It removes
the layers that duplicate each other: built-in plugins, sections, the
per-framework block folders, preset codes, the extra UI and API paths and the
split automation stack. Every phase is breaking, ships as its own pull
request, and comes with a codemod or a doctor lint. The terms are on the
[Naming](/docs/concepts/naming#glossary) page, and
[ADR 0093](https://github.com/ScaleDockHQ/better-supabase/blob/main/docs/decisions/0093-block-instances-and-one-data-contract.md)
records the decision.

1. **Terminology:** module becomes block, organization replaces tenant in
   every name you see, and `bs` becomes `bsb` everywhere.
2. **Config groups** (`database`, `auth`, `records`, `tables`, `blocks`,
   `adapters`, `gen`, `api`, `tooling`) and one organization resolver.
3. **The CLI** with one command per job, and a new `remove`.
4. **Designs replace preset codes** in Create URLs and the CLI.
5. **Block renames** to plain defaults: `tasks`, `contacts`, `activities`,
   `queue`, `automations`, `memberships`.
6. **Every block renameable,** and every primary noun a record type.
7. **Built-in plugins become block extensions.**
8. **One data contract** for app tables and block tables.
9. **Instances:** `block`, `on` and `uses` in config, SQL per instance,
   consumers that register, attach by default and one client per instance.
10. **The client and generated hooks,** with no transport or organization in
    app code.
11. **Per-framework block folders removed.**
12. **Record bindings** replace sections and record features.
13. **One UI path** through registry items.
14. **One API layer** and one spec entry, with supported and community
    adapters.
15. **One automation model** on the outbox and the queue.
16. **Wired by default:** env, routes, workers and providers from the
    manifests.
17. **Presets as products,** with `cms`, `erp` and `pim`.
18. **Gaps between blocks** people pick together.
19. **Local-first engines** behind the same hooks.

## Still open after quality, care and ERP

The blocks and presets of the 0.47 plan have shipped. What is left is the
follow-ups below, each of which becomes its own plan, plus the adapter
packages that live outside this repository.

### Care

- **Funding on the ledger and approvals:** awards and claims that post to
  `ledger`, and registrations that wait for an `approvals` request, plus a
  funding UI recipe. Today registration approval is the block's own status.
- **Read logging for direct selects:** the read log records reads through the
  blocks' read functions. A plain PostgREST select on a care table is not
  logged yet.
- **More care preset blocks:** `access-requests` and `invoices` as optional
  additions to the `care` preset.

### Deferred in the new blocks

- **Warehouse:** putaway and slotting rules, and route optimization of the
  pick path through the bins. Pick waves, pick lists and cycle counts ship in
  [warehouse](/docs/blocks/warehouse).
- **Maintenance:** `RRULE` recurrence for preventive plans (plans run by time
  or meter today), and links from a plan to the qualifications of its
  assets. See [maintenance](/docs/blocks/maintenance).
- **Meetings:** an exemption for `NO_FIELDS` on a meeting type. See
  [meetings](/docs/blocks/meetings).
- **Appraisals:** rubrics with several rated competencies per review, and
  reviewer anonymity. See [appraisals](/docs/blocks/appraisals).
- **Payroll export:** time entries are not filtered by timesheet approval,
  so unapproved hours count in a run. See
  [payroll export](/docs/blocks/payroll-export).
- **E-invoices:** `externalRef` is not persisted on the invoice, so a sent
  UBL document can't be matched back by it.
- **Tills:** the "Add a till" form in the example app has no cash account
  field. See [tills](/docs/blocks/tills).

### ERP

- **Drop ship receipts:** receiving a drop-ship purchase order fulfils the
  sales order it came from. Today the receipt leaves the sales order unfulfilled.
- **Credit limits on the order's customer:** the order credit limit and its
  override use the cart user's contacts person; they should use the
  customer the order is for.
- **Three-way match status per bill:** an invoices list function that
  returns the match status of each purchase bill, so a payables page shows it
  next to the aging totals.
- **Fuller UBL:** `toUbl` for invoices whose prices include VAT, and for
  charges added after issue, such as reminder fees.
- **Project revenue without a double count:** billed time and an invoice line
  with a `project` dimension both count as project revenue today, so
  [project costing](/docs/blocks/billable-time#project-costing) asks you to
  leave the dimension off lines that bill time.

### Adapter packages

These are external adapter repositories, not part of the library. They are
still to be written, and each plugs into a versioned port:

- iWlz, iJw and iWmo claims through `FundingClaimFormat`.
- The G-Standaard through `MedicationCatalog`.
- RGS chart of accounts imports.
- Peppol access points through `EInvoiceChannel`, with a UBL schema
  validator.
- Bank feeds through `BankFeed`.

### Platform and naming

- **The ADR 0150 follow-ups:** a pipeline stage move runs through the state
  machine when the record type has one, one SQL helper parses cron
  expressions for `ai-schedules`, `queue` and `workflows`, and an
  `automations` action starts a published `workflow-editor` workflow.
- **The remaining foreign key columns:** `rule_id` in the automation tables
  and `snapshot_id` in forecasts take their entity's name, as `task_id` and
  `activity_id` did in 0.47.
- **Adopted tables in `upgrade`:** an adopted inbox `contacts` table, an
  adopted `members` table for sequences and an adopted
  `ai_document_versions` table are copied by hand today. `upgrade` should
  move them as it moves the block's own tables.
- **Payload filters on subscriptions:** a webhook endpoint or outbox consumer
  that subscribes to `enrollment.*` gets every program's events. A filter on
  `data.program` would let it keep only `sequence:` runs.

### Removed in 0.48

0.48 drops what 0.47 keeps for one minor. These are still in the code today:
the old entity keys and subject types, the outbox `legacyEventTypes` option
and `SequenceEnrollmentEventData`, and the 0.46 permission keys in configured
roles, `DbError.legacyHint` and `workItemChecklistBinding` go with them. The
[upgrade guide](/docs/migration/0.46-to-0.47#removed-in-048) lists each one
with its replacement.

## Later

  the shape of `powersyncEngine` and `sqliteEngine`.
  Upstream: `electric-sql/electric`, `rocicorp/mono`.
