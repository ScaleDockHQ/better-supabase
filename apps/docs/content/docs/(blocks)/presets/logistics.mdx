---
title: Logistics and fleet
description: The logistics preset installs shipments with carrier rates, labels and tracking, orders to ship, customers as parties, and warehouses and vehicles in assets.
---

The `logistics` preset is for an app in the style of Shippo or a fleet
tracker. An order is packed into a shipment, the app asks your carriers for
rates and buys a label, and the tracking timeline follows the package to
delivery. Warehouses and vehicles are assets, so a shipment can name the
location it ships from.

## Config

```ts title="better-supabase.config.ts"
import { defineConfig } from "better-supabase/config";

export default defineConfig({ preset: "logistics" });
```

`npx better-supabase init --preset logistics` starts a new project from it,
and `npx better-supabase add --preset logistics` adds it to one you have
([CLI](/docs/cli/presets)).

## Blocks

The preset is [`saas`](/docs/presets#named-presets) plus these blocks, and
whatever they require:

| Block                               | Role in the app                                   |
| ----------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------- |
| [parties](/docs/blocks/contacts)    | Shippers, consignees and carriers' accounts       |
| [orders](/docs/blocks/orders)       | The orders that ship                              |
| [shipments](/docs/blocks/shipments) | Packages, rates, labels and the tracking timeline |
| [assets](/docs/blocks/assets)       | Warehouses, docks and vehicles                    |

## How the blocks connect

A shipment is for a record, such as an order, and ships from an asset
location to an address. Carriers plug in through the versioned
`ShippingProvider` interface: you register one per carrier you use, and the
block asks each for rates and buys the label from the one you pick. The
status follows the latest tracking event, whether it came from a carrier
poll, a signed webhook or a person, and a repeated webhook adds no duplicate
([shipments](/docs/blocks/shipments)).

## With ReUI

- Data Grid for shipments, with Filters on status, carrier and date.
- Timeline for a shipment's tracking events.
- A rate picker as a Radio Group of carrier services and prices.
