title: Compliance
description: The compliance preset installs frameworks and controls with recurring evidence tasks, evidence files, versioned policies that members acknowledge and a posture per framework.
---

The `compliance` preset is for an app in the style of Vanta or Drata. A
company maps its controls to a framework, collects evidence for each control
on a schedule, publishes policies that every employee acknowledges, and
shows an auditor the evidence trail.

## Config

```ts title="better-supabase.config.ts"
import { defineConfig } from "better-supabase/config";

export default defineConfig({ preset: "compliance" });
```

`npx better-supabase init --preset compliance` starts a new project from it,
and `npx better-supabase add --preset compliance` adds it to one you have
([CLI](/docs/cli/presets)).

## Blocks

The preset is [`saas`](/docs/presets#named-presets) plus these blocks, and
whatever they require:

| Block                                   | Role in the app                                             |
| --------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------- |
| [compliance](/docs/blocks/compliance)   | Frameworks, controls, evidence tasks and versioned policies |
| [attachments](/docs/blocks/attachments) | Evidence files                                              |

The compliance block requires [versions](/docs/blocks/versions) and
[acknowledgements](/docs/blocks/acknowledgements), which the preset installs
with it, and the `audit` block from `saas` keeps who changed
a control or its evidence.

## How the blocks connect

A control belongs to a framework such as SOC 2 or ISO 27001. `addEvidence`
attaches a file or a note to it, valid until a date, and `saveEvidenceTask`
plans evidence that has to be collected again every `intervalDays`, with an
assignee. A control's status follows from its evidence and tasks: it fails
when a task is overdue or all its evidence has lapsed, and `posture` rolls
the statuses up per framework. `publishPolicy` freezes a policy as a version,
and each member acknowledges that version through the acknowledgements
block's `acknowledge` ([compliance](/docs/blocks/compliance#acknowledgements)).

## With ReUI

- Data Grid for controls, with their status as a Badge and Filters on framework.
- Progress for each framework's readiness.
- Timeline for a control's evidence.
