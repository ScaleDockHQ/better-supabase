---
title: Field service
description: The field-service preset installs a dispatch board of work orders, customer sites in assets, parts from inventory, quotes to invoices, checklists and signatures.
---

The `field-service` preset is for an app in the style of ServiceTitan or
Jobber. The office quotes a job, a dispatcher puts the work order in a
technician's slot on the board, and the technician works through the day
plan, fills in the checklist on site, records the parts used and has the
customer sign.

## Config

```ts title="better-supabase.config.ts"
import { defineConfig } from "better-supabase/config";

export default defineConfig({ preset: "field-service" });
```

`npx better-supabase init --preset field-service` starts a new project from
it, and `npx better-supabase add --preset field-service` adds it to one you
have ([CLI](/docs/cli/presets)).

## Blocks

The preset is [`saas`](/docs/presets#named-presets) plus these blocks, and
whatever they require:

| Block                                   | Role in the app                                               |
| --------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------- |
| [parties](/docs/blocks/contacts)        | Customers and their contacts                                  |
| [dispatch](/docs/blocks/dispatch)       | Work orders, the schedule board, day plans and parts used     |
| [assets](/docs/blocks/assets)           | Customer sites and the equipment installed there              |
| [inventory](/docs/blocks/inventory)     | Van and warehouse stock the parts come from                   |
| [quotes](/docs/blocks/quotes)           | Quotes the customer accepts through a public link             |
| [invoices](/docs/blocks/invoices)       | Invoices from accepted quotes, with payments                  |
| [forms](/docs/blocks/forms)             | Job checklists and inspection forms                           |
| signatures                              | The customer's signature on a frozen version of the job sheet |
| [attachments](/docs/blocks/attachments) | Photos before and after the work                              |

## How the blocks connect

A work order sits at an asset location or an address. `board` returns the
scheduled work orders per technician and day with the backlog, and
`technicianDay` returns one technician's jobs in route order. The
technician moves a job to en route, on site and done, and records each part
with the catalog variant and stock location it came from, since `catalog`
and `inventory` are installed ([dispatch](/docs/blocks/dispatch)).

A quote the customer accepts becomes an invoice. The checklist is a form
submission linked to the work order, and the signature is taken on a frozen
version, so what the customer signed never changes.

## With ReUI

- Event Calendar for the dispatch board, one row per technician.
- Data Grid for the backlog, with Filters on priority and customer.
- Stepper for the on-site flow: checklist, parts, photos, signature.
