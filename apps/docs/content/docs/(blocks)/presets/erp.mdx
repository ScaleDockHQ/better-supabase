title: ERP

---

The `erp` preset is for a small ERP in the style of Odoo or NetSuite: sell
from a catalog, keep stock per location, buy from suppliers, quote and
invoice customers, keep the books and reconcile the bank, and track the
people who do the work.

## Config

```ts title="better-supabase.config.ts"
import { defineConfig } from "better-supabase/config";

export default defineConfig({ preset: "erp" });
```

`npx better-supabase init --preset erp` starts a new project from it, and
`npx better-supabase add --preset erp` adds it to one you have
([CLI](/docs/cli/presets)). To make things as well, start from the
[`manufacturing`](/docs/presets/manufacturing) preset, which is `erp` plus
production.

## Blocks

The preset is [`saas`](/docs/presets#named-presets) plus these blocks, and
whatever they require:

| Block                                       | Role in the app                                                         |
| ------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| [contacts](/docs/blocks/contacts)           | Customers and suppliers, with payment terms and credit limits           |
| [catalog](/docs/blocks/catalog)             | Products, variants and price lists                                      |
| [inventory](/docs/blocks/inventory)         | Stock per location, purchase requisitions, purchase orders and receipts |
| [assets](/docs/blocks/assets)               | The warehouses stock is kept at, and fixed assets that depreciate       |
| [quotes](/docs/blocks/quotes)               | Quotes for a customer, priced from the catalog                          |
| [orders](/docs/blocks/orders)               | Orders, their payment and fulfilment, and drop ship                     |
| [invoices](/docs/blocks/invoices)           | Sales invoices and purchase bills, with their payments                  |
| [accounting](/docs/blocks/accounting)       | The chart of accounts, journals, periods and reports                    |
| [banking](/docs/blocks/banking)             | Bank accounts, imported statements and their reconciliation             |
| [approvals](/docs/blocks/approvals)         | The decision on purchase requisitions and expense reports               |
| [numbering](/docs/blocks/numbering)         | Gapless sequences for your own document numbers                         |
| [employees](/docs/blocks/employees)         | The org chart, with each member's employee record                       |
| [time-tracking](/docs/blocks/time-tracking) | Timesheets with weekly approval                                         |
| [leave](/docs/blocks/leave)                 | Leave requests and balances                                             |
| [expenses](/docs/blocks/expenses)           | Expense reports with receipts, policies and approval                    |
| [saved-views](/docs/blocks/saved-views)     | Saved filters for each list                                             |
| [automations](/docs/blocks/automations)     | The default automations, which members can edit                         |

Each organization runs `db.accounting.setup(organizationId)` once before
anything books: it writes the accounting settings and seeds the generic
chart and the `SAL`, `PUR`, `BNK`, `CSH` and `GEN` journals
([setup](/docs/blocks/accounting#setup-and-the-chart)). Until then the other
blocks work and book nothing.

## How the blocks connect

The blocks pick each other up when they are installed together, with no
config.

### Selling

- A quote line that names a catalog variant takes its price and title when
  it leaves them out ([quotes](/docs/blocks/quotes)). A line with no price
  for the quote's currency fails with `LINES_INVALID`.
- An accepted quote becomes an order: the preset turns on the
  `quotes.quote_to_order` default automation, which ships turned off
  ([default automations](/docs/blocks/automations#default-automations)).
  Register `orders.from_quote` in the rules consumer's action registry, or
  turn it off with `disable: ["quotes.quote_to_order"]` in the automations
  options.
- A paid order creates, issues and pays an invoice and returns its id
  ([orders](/docs/blocks/orders)). Orders paid before invoices was installed
  have no invoice.
- Placing an order and issuing a sales invoice compare what the customer
  already owes plus the new total with the party's credit limit, and fail
  with `ORDER_CREDIT_LIMIT_EXCEEDED` or `INVOICE_CREDIT_LIMIT_EXCEEDED`
  unless the caller holds `orders.override_credit`
  ([credit limits](/docs/blocks/invoices#credit-limits)).

### Buying

- A purchase requisition (`requestPurchase`) opens an approval request, and
  a holder of `inventory.purchase` decides it unless the organization's
  policy names other approvers
  ([requisitions](/docs/blocks/inventory#requisitions)).
  `purchaseOrderFromRequisitions` turns approved ones into a purchase order,
  and a blocked supplier can't get one (`PURCHASE_ORDER_SUPPLIER_BLOCKED`).
- Receiving a purchase order writes an `inventory_receipts` row with its
  lines and adds the stock at its cost.
- A purchase bill is an invoice with `direction: "purchase"` and the
  purchase order's id. `db.invoices.match(billId)` runs the three-way match
  of the bill against the order and what arrived, and paying a bill that
  doesn't match fails with `INVOICE_MATCH_FAILED` unless the caller holds
  `invoices.override_match`
  ([the three-way match](/docs/blocks/invoices#the-three-way-match)).
- `purchaseOrderFromOrder(orderId, supplier)` buys an order's lines for drop
  ship, with the order's shipping address as the purchase order's `ship_to`.

### The books

Each block books its documents in the accounting journals in the same
transaction as the write that changes them
([postings](/docs/blocks/accounting#postings-from-other-blocks)):

| Document                | Journal  | Entry                                                                    |
| ----------------------- | -------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Sales invoice           | sales    | the receivable against revenue and tax per rate                          |
| Invoice payment         | bank     | the bank against the receivable                                          |
| Purchase bill           | purchase | the payable against expense and deductible tax, or the receipt's payable |
| Stock receipt and issue | general  | inventory at average cost against payable or cost of goods               |
| Approved expense report | purchase | the expense against the payable to the member                            |
| Month of depreciation   | general  | depreciation expense against accumulated depreciation                    |

A product line of a bill with a purchase order debits the payable the stock
receipt already booked, so received stock isn't counted twice. Voiding a
document reverses its entry. Entries post at once into an open period after
the lock date, and wait as drafts for an accountant otherwise.

### The bank

- A bank account names the bank journal it books to. Statements import from
  camt.053 or MT940 files, or arrive through a `BankFeed`
  ([accounts and statements](/docs/blocks/banking#accounts-and-statements)).
- `suggestMatches(transactionId)` lists the open invoices a line may pay, by
  creditor reference, the invoice number in the remittance, the amount and
  the counterparty's IBAN. Matching a line records an invoice payment, so
  the invoice is paid and the payment books in the bank journal
  ([matching](/docs/blocks/banking#matching)).
- Bank rules book recurring lines such as fees against an account of their
  own.
- `reconciliation(accountId)` compares the latest statement's closing
  balance with the ledger balance of the journal's account, and
  `reconciled` is true once nothing is unmatched and the two agree
  ([reconciliation](/docs/blocks/banking#reconciliation)). Closing a period
  lists unreconciled bank lines among its blockers.

### People

- Inventory keeps its locations in the assets registry, so a warehouse is
  an asset ([inventory](/docs/blocks/inventory)).
- Time entries and leave requests carry the `employee_id` of their member,
  which employees keeps current when a member's employee record changes
  ([employees](/docs/blocks/employees#time-and-leave)).
- Expense reports go through approvals, and an approved report books in the
  purchase journal; banking can match its reimbursement.

## With ReUI

Each part of the app has a recipe built from ReUI components, or uses one
directly:

- [Invoices table](/docs/registry/ui-recipes/invoices-table): a Data Grid
  with Filters on status and party. Purchase bills use the same grid with a
  Badge for each bill's three-way match.
- [Bank reconciliation](/docs/registry/ui-recipes/bank-reconciliation): one
  card per account with the statement and ledger balances, and its lines
  with the suggested invoice.
- [Trial balance](/docs/registry/ui-recipes/trial-balance): the balance sheet
  totals above the posted balances per account.
- Kanban for open quotes by status.

The example app shows the trial balance under `/finance`, what you owe
suppliers by days past due under `/finance/payables`, and the reconciliation
under `/banking`.
