---
title: Composing blocks
description: Where the glue between blocks lives, from record pages and lists to flows between blocks and forms with custom fields.
---

Blocks work together through three layers. Knowing which layer a piece of
glue belongs to tells you where to write it and what `gen` writes for you.

| Layer                    | Owner           | What it holds                                                             |
| ------------------------ | --------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Library logic            | better-supabase | record bindings, record lists, the timeline, My work, flows, `recordForm` |
| One file per record type | `gen`           | the record type that names the blocks bound to it                         |
| UI                       | your app        | recipes copied into the app and rendered with your shadcn components      |

## Reads: record bindings

A record binding is one block's part of a record, such as its comments, files
or versions, with a `load`, an optional `count` and a permission, named by the
block's key in the config. With `gen.recordTypes`, `gen` writes one record type
per type in `config.records` with the blocks bound to it. Everything that shows
more than one block on a record reads bindings:

- a [record page](/docs/blocks/records) loads each binding on its own, so a
  failing or hidden one doesn't take the page down;
- a [record list](/docs/blocks/record-lists) fills columns from a binding's
  `count` or rows, one query per page;
- the timeline merges the bindings of one record, newest first;
- My work collects what waits on the caller across the installed blocks.

## Writes: flows

A [flow](/docs/blocks/flows) turns an event in one block into a record in
another, once per source record, and keeps the lineage as a `created_from`
relation. Turn on a built-in such as `conversationToWorkItem` by listing it
in `createFlows`, or write your own with `defineFlow`.

## Forms: entity schema and custom fields

[`recordForm`](/docs/blocks/entity-forms) builds one form from a block's
entity schema and the organization's custom fields, and `useRecordForm`
holds its state. The form recipe renders it.

## Which layer?

- Data that belongs to one record and shows on its page or list: a record binding.
- A record that should exist because another one changed: a flow.
- A form over a block entity with tenant fields: `recordForm` and the recipe.
- Markup of any kind: a [recipe](/docs/registry/ui-recipes), never the library.

The decision is ADR 0064; ADR 0093 replaces its sections with record bindings.
