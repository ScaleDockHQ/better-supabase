---
title: Nonprofit fundraising
description: The nonprofit preset installs donors, donations and recurring gifts on the ledger, campaign targets and saved views for donor segments.
---

The `nonprofit` preset is for an app in the style of Bloomerang. A
fundraiser keeps donors, records one-off and recurring gifts against a
campaign, follows progress towards the campaign goal and sends tax receipts.

## Config

```ts title="better-supabase.config.ts"
import { defineConfig } from "better-supabase/config";

export default defineConfig({ preset: "nonprofit" });
```

`npx better-supabase init --preset nonprofit` starts a new project from it,
and `npx better-supabase apply --preset nonprofit` adds it to one you have
([CLI](/docs/cli/presets)).

## Blocks

The preset is [`saas`](/docs/presets#named-presets) plus these blocks, and
whatever they require:

| Block                                   | Role in the app                                    |
| --------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------- |
| [parties](/docs/blocks/parties)         | Donors, households and organizations               |
| [donations](/docs/blocks/donations)     | Gifts, recurring gifts, campaigns and tax receipts |
| [ledger](/docs/blocks/ledger)           | The funds each gift is posted to                   |
| [targets](/docs/blocks/targets)         | Campaign goals per period                          |
| [saved-views](/docs/blocks/saved-views) | Donor segments, such as lapsed or major donors     |

## How the blocks connect

A donation names its donor in `parties` and its campaign, and posts to a
fund on the ledger, so fund balances are ledger balances. A campaign goal is
a target whose progress the app keeps from the campaign's donations. A
segment is a saved view on donors with a filter tree.

## With ReUI

- Data Grid for donors and gifts, with Filters for segments.
- Progress for each campaign towards its goal.
- Statistic cards for the period's total, donors and average gift.
