The `nonprofit` preset is for an app in the style of Bloomerang. A
fundraiser keeps donors, records one-off gifts, charges monthly pledges
through a payments provider, follows each fund towards its goal and issues
numbered tax receipts.

## Config

```ts title="better-supabase.config.ts"
import { defineConfig } from "better-supabase/config";

export default defineConfig({ preset: "nonprofit" });
```

`npx better-supabase init --preset nonprofit` starts a new project from it,
and `npx better-supabase apply --preset nonprofit` adds it to one you have
([CLI](/docs/cli/presets)).

## Blocks

The preset is [`saas`](/docs/presets#named-presets) plus these blocks, and
whatever they require:

| Block                                             | Role in the app                                 |
| ------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------- |
| [parties](/docs/blocks/parties)                   | Donors, households and organizations            |
| [donations](/docs/blocks/donations)               | Funds, gifts, pledges and tax receipts          |
| [ledger](/docs/blocks/ledger)                     | The income account each gift posts to, per fund |
| [number-sequences](/docs/blocks/number-sequences) | Receipt numbers in your own format              |
| [targets](/docs/blocks/targets)                   | Fundraising targets per team or period          |
| [saved-views](/docs/blocks/saved-views)           | Donor segments, such as lapsed or major donors  |

## How the blocks connect

A gift names its donor, any record in `config.subjects` such as a party, and
posts on the ledger to its fund, so what a fund raised is a ledger balance
and `funds()` returns it next to the goal. A pledge repeats a gift every
week, month, quarter or year: run `chargeDuePledges` from a scheduled job and
it charges each due installment once through the versioned `PaymentProvider`
interface, and the provider's webhook settles the gift. `issueGiftReceipt`
and `issueYearlyReceipt` number receipts with the `donation_receipt` format
from number-sequences ([donations](/docs/blocks/donations)).

## With ReUI

- Data Grid for donors and gifts, with Filters for segments.
- Progress for each fund towards its goal.
- Statistic cards for the year's total, donors and average gift.
