The `contracts` preset is for an app in the style of Ironclad. Legal drafts a
contract with a counterparty, it goes through approval, both sides sign, and
the app then tracks the obligations it creates and warns before it renews or
expires.

## Config

```ts title="better-supabase.config.ts"
import { defineConfig } from "better-supabase/config";

export default defineConfig({ preset: "contracts" });
```

`npx better-supabase init --preset contracts` starts a new project from it,
and `npx better-supabase apply --preset contracts` adds it to one you have
([CLI](/docs/cli/presets)).

## Blocks

The preset is [`saas`](/docs/presets#named-presets) plus these blocks, and
whatever they require:

| Block                               | Role in the app                                      |
| ----------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------- |
| [contracts](/docs/blocks/contracts) | Contracts, their lifecycle, renewals and obligations |
| [parties](/docs/blocks/parties)     | Counterparties                                       |
| signatures                          | Signing on a frozen version of the contract          |
| [versions](/docs/blocks/versions)   | Drafts and redlines as numbered versions             |
| [approvals](/docs/blocks/approvals) | Legal and finance approval before signing            |
| [reminders](/docs/blocks/reminders) | Renewal and obligation due dates                     |

## How the blocks connect

A contract moves from draft through approval and signature to active, then
renews or ends. The approved draft is a frozen version, and the signatures
are taken on that version, so what was signed never changes. Renewal
notices and obligation due dates are reminders delivered through
notifications.

## With ReUI

- Data Grid for contracts, with Filters on counterparty, status and end date.
- Timeline for a contract's versions, approvals and signatures.
- Calendar view of upcoming renewals.
