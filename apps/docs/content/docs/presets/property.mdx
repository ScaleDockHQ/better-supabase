title: Property management

The `property` preset is for an app in the style of AppFolio or Buildium. A
property manager keeps buildings and units, signs leases with tenants, bills
the rent every period and handles maintenance requests, and each tenant signs
in to a portal that shows their own lease, invoices and requests.

## Config

```ts title="better-supabase.config.ts"
import { defineConfig } from "better-supabase/config";

export default defineConfig({ preset: "property" });
```

`npx better-supabase init --preset property` starts a new project from it,
and `npx better-supabase apply --preset property` adds it to one you have
([CLI](/docs/cli/presets)).

## Blocks

The preset is [`saas`](/docs/presets#named-presets) plus these blocks, and
whatever they require:

| Block                                   | Role in the app                                                              |
| --------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| [assets](/docs/blocks/assets)           | Sites, buildings and units as a location hierarchy, and appliances in them   |
| [leases](/docs/blocks/leases)           | Leases on units with their tenants, terms and recurring charges              |
| [ledger](/docs/blocks/ledger)           | The account each lease posts its charges, payments and deposits to           |
| [parties](/docs/blocks/parties)         | Tenants, owners and vendors                                                  |
| [guests](/docs/blocks/guests)           | The tenant portal: a tenant reads the lease, invoices and requests they hold |
| [invoices](/docs/blocks/invoices)       | Rent invoices with payments and overdue aging                                |
| [work-items](/docs/blocks/work-items)   | Maintenance requests, with the unit or asset they affect                     |
| [attachments](/docs/blocks/attachments) | Signed leases, inspection photos and receipts                                |
| [reminders](/docs/blocks/reminders)     | Lease renewals and inspections                                               |

## How the blocks connect

A lease points at a unit in `assets` and a tenant in `parties`. Its
recurring charges post to the lease's account on the ledger, so the balance
a tenant owes is a ledger balance, never a separate column. Rent invoices
come from those charges, and a payment on an invoice posts back through the
ledger.

A maintenance request is a work item that names the affected unit or asset.
The tenant is a guest tied to their party, so the portal shows the records
granted to that party and nothing else in the organization.

## With ReUI

- Data Grid for units and leases, with Filters on occupancy and the lease end.
- Kanban for maintenance requests by status.
- Timeline for a lease's charges and payments, newest first.
