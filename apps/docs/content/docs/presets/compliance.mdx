title: Compliance
description: The compliance preset installs controls with recurring evidence, evidence files, policy documents with acknowledgements and reminders.
---

The `compliance` preset is for an app in the style of Vanta or Drata. A
company maps its controls to a framework, collects evidence for each control
on a schedule, publishes policies that every employee acknowledges, and
shows an auditor the evidence trail.

## Config

```ts title="better-supabase.config.ts"
import { defineConfig } from "better-supabase/config";

export default defineConfig({ preset: "compliance" });
```

`npx better-supabase init --preset compliance` starts a new project from it,
and `npx better-supabase apply --preset compliance` adds it to one you have
([CLI](/docs/cli/presets)).

## Blocks

The preset is [`saas`](/docs/presets#named-presets) plus these blocks, and
whatever they require:

| Block                                   | Role in the app                                   |
| --------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------- |
| [controls](/docs/blocks/controls)       | Controls, frameworks and recurring evidence tasks |
| [attachments](/docs/blocks/attachments) | Evidence files                                    |
| [documents](/docs/blocks/documents)     | Policies, written together                        |
| signatures                              | Policy acknowledgements on a frozen version       |
| [reminders](/docs/blocks/reminders)     | Evidence due dates and policy reviews             |

The `audit` block from `saas` keeps who changed a control or its evidence.

## How the blocks connect

A control has evidence tasks that recur, and each piece of evidence is an
attachment on the task. A policy is a document; publishing it freezes a
version, and each employee signs that version as their acknowledgement.
Reminders warn the owner before evidence or a policy review is due.

## With ReUI

- Data Grid for controls, with their status as a Badge and Filters on framework.
- Progress for each framework's readiness.
- Timeline for a control's evidence.
