The roadmap groups work by horizon. Each item names the upstream project it
follows and the subpath where it lands, so you can tell which import changes
when it ships. Items move between horizons as upstream releases land; the
[changelog](/docs/changelog)
records what shipped.

## Shipped in 0.8

The [0.8.0 changelog](/docs/changelog#080-2026-10-12)
has the details.

- `audience` and `issuer` pinning in `better-supabase/server`
  ([token audience](/docs/auth/server#token-audience)).
- The `standardSchema()` generator in `better-supabase/config`.
- TanStack DB collections with Realtime sync in
  `better-supabase/tanstack-db/supabase`.
- Typed Edge Function calls with `defineFunction()` and `functions()`.
- Supabase MCP server interop with `supabaseMcp()` and `supabaseMcpHandler()`.
- Supabase Lite in `better-supabase/server`, `better-supabase/testing` and the
  CLI.

## Now

Shipping in 0.9.

- **Exact money on the wire** (the deals, quotes, invoices, pipelines and
  employees blocks, and `db.$rpc`): amounts are `Money` values, every `int8`
  crosses PostgREST as text and decodes strictly, and doctor BS223 flags
  functions that return a bare `bigint` or `numeric`. See
  [ADR 0037](https://github.com/ScaleDockHQ/better-supabase/blob/main/docs/decisions/0037-money-on-the-wire.md).
- **Courses and scored forms** (`better-supabase/blocks/courses` and
  `better-supabase/blocks/forms`): courses of modules and lessons on
  enrollments, quizzes graded in SQL, certificates, and NPS rollups per form.
  See [Courses](/docs/blocks/courses).
- **Posts** (`better-supabase/blocks/posts`): forum and feedback boards on
  the messaging core, with votes, roadmap statuses, merged duplicates,
  replies and moderation. See [Posts](/docs/blocks/posts).
- **Vertical presets** (`better-supabase/preset`): nineteen presets in a
  Verticals group, from property management on leases to community and
  product feedback on posts, each `saas` plus the blocks of one kind of
  business app. See [Presets](/docs/presets#vertical-presets).

## Next

Parity gaps with what Supabase ships today.

- **Compiled JSON Schemas** (the `jsonb-schemas` SQL module). CHECK
  constraints use `pg_jsonschema`'s compiled `jsonschema` type once the
  platform ships a version that has it; Supabase runs 0.3.3, which has
  `jsonschema_validation_errors` but no compiled type. Upstream:
  `supabase/pg_jsonschema`.

## Planned: collaboration and SaaS parity

This track adds one messaging core with a team chat block on it, moves
comments and the inbox onto that core, and then closes the feature gaps
against CRMs (Salesforce, HubSpot, Attio, Apollo), Google Workspace and
thirty other SaaS verticals. Each phase ships as its own pull request, and
the breaking phases ship with a data migration, an upgrade guide and a
changeset that lists every old name next to its replacement.
[ADR 0062](https://github.com/ScaleDockHQ/better-supabase/blob/main/docs/decisions/0062-messaging-core-and-breaking-moves.md)
records the messaging core and
[ADR 0063](https://github.com/ScaleDockHQ/better-supabase/blob/main/docs/decisions/0063-activity-block.md)
records the activity block.

better-supabase stands on each:

| Team chat (channels, DMs, threads, unread, typing)   | the `messaging` and `channels` blocks                                                          | nothing new                                                          |

Twenty more verticals (property management, field service, clinics,
courses, events, legal, expenses, contracts, customer success, communities,
product feedback, call intelligence, logistics, restaurants, fitness,
nonprofits, IT service management, compliance, construction and insurance)
need the same few primitives: a ledger, assets and locations, votes and
scores, moderation, enrollments, guest and portal access, check-in passes,
merge and anchors on files. Those become shared blocks first, and the
verticals become thin blocks and presets on top of them.

### Phases

1. **Table and entity renames in `upgrade`:** renaming a block's tables
   or entities on an installed project emits `alter table ... rename` and
   function swaps, plus the column moves a module declares in
   `NAMES.migrations`.
2. **Entities for the collaboration blocks:** comments, attachments,
   reactions, engagements, documents, calendar and reminders declare
   renameable entities and `schemas`, and the hand-written subject cases go.
3. **One `RecordRef` (breaking):** every subject block takes the subject as
   its first argument, reads the tenant from the block environment and
   returns comment and file authors. `createBlockClient` replaces the
   per-block `connect*` wrappers.
4. **Permissions and subjects on the client:** `my_permissions`,
   `permissionFlags`, `can`, `usePermission` and `Can`; `subject_titles`
   and `resolveSubjects`; URLs for every subject.
5. **Sections:** `defineSection` in block manifests, `loadSections`,
   `record_summary`, tenant-scoped cache tags and `createRecordController`.
6. **Generated features:** `gen` writes one binding per subject with a
   section per installed block. UI stays recipes only.
7. **Lists, workspace, flows and forms:** `defineRecordList`, `my_work`,
   generated navigation and commands, `defineFlow` and `recordForm`.
8. **The messaging core and the `channels` block:** conversations,
   messages, edits, members, thread reads, follows, mentions, typing and
   scheduled send, with attachments and reactions on messages, and a `chat`
   preset.
9. **Comments on the core (breaking):** comments become subject
   conversations with resolve, edit history, pins, anchors and attachments,
   and the activity feed moves to its own `activity` block.
10. **Tasks and files:** attachment versions and previews, checklists,
    recurrence and watchers on work items.
11. **The inbox on the core (breaking):** inbox conversations and messages
    move onto the core, inbound files become scanned attachments, and the
    `crm` preset gains deals, quotes and targets.
12. **Shared collaboration features:** presence, record mentions, save for
    later and AI thread summaries.
13. **CRM gaps:** mail sync, email templates and tracking, sequences,
    segments, assignment and territories, enrichment, lead scoring,
    forecast snapshots and field history.
14. **Google Workspace parity:** calendar availability, appointment
    schedules, inbox snooze and scheduled send, document suggestion mode and
    drive labels and approvals.
15. **Shared primitives (breaking where they absorb existing tables):**
    ledger, assets and locations, votes and scores, moderation, enrollments,
    guest and portal access, merge, file anchors, passes and billable time.
16. **Community and feedback:** the `posts` block, and the `community` and
    `feedback` presets on it. Shipped; see [Posts](/docs/blocks/posts) and
    [Presets](/docs/presets#vertical-presets).

## Planned: block instances and one data contract

Picking blocks should build a working product. This track gives every
concept one name, lets you install a block more than once under its own
names, attaches blocks to your record types by default, and puts app tables
and block tables behind the same data verbs and generated hooks. It removes
the layers that duplicate each other: built-in plugins, sections, the
per-framework block folders, preset codes, the extra UI and API paths and the
split automation stack. Every phase is breaking, ships as its own pull
request, and comes with a codemod or a doctor lint. The terms are on the
[Naming](/docs/concepts/naming#glossary) page, and
[ADR 0093](https://github.com/ScaleDockHQ/better-supabase/blob/main/docs/decisions/0093-block-instances-and-one-data-contract.md)
records the decision.

1. **Terminology:** module becomes block, organization replaces tenant in
   every name you see, and `bs` becomes `bsb` everywhere.
2. **Config groups** (`database`, `auth`, `records`, `tables`, `blocks`,
   `adapters`, `gen`, `api`, `tooling`) and one organization resolver.
3. **The CLI** with one command per job, and a new `remove`.
4. **Designs replace preset codes** in Create URLs and the CLI.
5. **Block renames** to plain defaults: `tasks`, `contacts`, `activities`,
   `queue`, `automations`, `memberships`.
6. **Every block renameable,** and every primary noun a record type.
7. **Built-in plugins become block extensions.**
8. **One data contract** for app tables and block tables.
9. **Instances:** `block`, `on` and `uses` in config, SQL per instance,
   consumers that register, attach by default and one client per instance.
10. **The client and generated hooks,** with no transport or organization in
    app code.
11. **Per-framework block folders removed.**
12. **Record bindings** replace sections and record features.
13. **One UI path** through registry items.
14. **One API layer** and one spec entry, with supported and community
    adapters.
15. **One automation model** on the outbox and the queue.
16. **Wired by default:** env, routes, workers and providers from the
    manifests.
17. **Presets as products,** with `cms`, `erp` and `pim`.
18. **Gaps between blocks** people pick together.
19. **Local-first engines** behind the same hooks.

## Later

- **A machine-readable capability file** for better-supabase itself, in the
  shape of the `supabase/sdk` capability matrix. Upstream: `supabase/sdk`.
- **ElectricSQL and Zero collections**: executors and TanStack DB
  collections over their sync engines, after `better-supabase/expo-sqlite`
  and `better-supabase/tanstack-db` settle the executor interface.
  Upstream: `electric-sql/electric`, `rocicorp/mono`.
