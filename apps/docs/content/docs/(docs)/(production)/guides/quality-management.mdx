---
title: Quality management
description: Set up an ISO 9001 quality system with the qms preset, from controlled documents and acknowledgements to nonconformities, audits, risks and qualifications.
---

The `qms` preset installs the blocks an ISO 9001 quality system needs, and
links them through [relations](/docs/blocks/relations) so an auditor can follow
a requirement from the document to the case, the action and the person who
read it. This guide walks the clauses in order and names the block behind each
one.

```bash
better-supabase init --preset qms
```

Open the [qms preset page](/docs/presets/qms) for the full block list. The
`ehs` and `isms` presets reuse the same blocks with other kinds and checklists.

## Controlled documents (clause 7.5)

Policies, procedures and work instructions live in [drive](/docs/blocks/drive)
as controlled documents. Each change publishes a new version with
[versions](/docs/blocks/versions), needs [approvals](/docs/blocks/approvals)
before it is effective, and carries a [signature](/docs/blocks/signatures) from
the approver. Numbers such as `SOP-014` come from
[numbering](/docs/blocks/numbering).

## Awareness and competence (clauses 7.2 and 7.3)

[Acknowledgements](/docs/blocks/acknowledgements) ask people to confirm they
read a new document version, and keep who confirmed and when.
[Qualifications](/docs/blocks/qualifications) record the training, licences
and certificates each person holds with their expiry dates, and
[courses](/docs/blocks/courses) deliver the training that earns them.

## Risks and opportunities (clause 6.1)

[Risks](/docs/blocks/risks) keeps the register: each risk has an owner, a
likelihood and an impact, and its treatments are work items. Link a risk to the
process or document it concerns with a relation so the register and the
documents stay connected.

## Internal audit (clause 9.2)

[Inspections](/docs/blocks/inspections) plans an audit programme. Each audit
has a lead, a team and a checklist form, and its findings are typed as
nonconformities, observations or opportunities. A nonconformity opens a case
before the audit can close.

## Nonconformity and corrective action (clause 10.2)

[Cases](/docs/blocks/cases) tracks nonconformities, complaints and changes from
the report to a verified close. Each kind has its own pipeline, a root cause
and actions, and closes only after the effectiveness check its kind requires.
A case names the documents it affects, so a corrective action that changes a
procedure points at the new version.

## Review and evidence

The audit log records every change, and the data exporter writes it as CSV for
the management review. The example app groups these pages under a Quality
section in the navigation: compliance, qualifications, risks, cases and
inspections.
